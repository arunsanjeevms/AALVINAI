Overview

What Vasool does

Vasool is a mobile collection app for microfinance field teams. Agents record customer payments as they collect them and print receipts on the spot from a Bluetooth POS printer, while payment tracking keeps each customer’s account up to date.

Vasool is one of five products in AALVINAI’s Finance, Accounting & Financial Services line-up, alongside Credixa, ChitFlow, Billora and PayFlow — and like every AALVINAI product it can run on its own or with the rest of the stack.
