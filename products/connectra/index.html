Overview

What Connectra does

Connectra is a CRM that keeps every customer relationship in one place: customer profiles, lead tracking, sales pipelines, follow-ups and a full history of every interaction.

Connectra is one of five products in AALVINAI’s Business Management & Enterprise Productivity line-up, alongside PeopleHub, DocuFlow, HelpNest and InsightIQ — and like every AALVINAI product it can run on its own or with the rest of the stack.
