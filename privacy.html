Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 · AALVINAI Technologies

1. Overview & Commitment

At AALVINAI Technologies, we engineer intelligent enterprise platforms, financial infrastructure, and AI solutions with uncompromising security and confidentiality. This policy outlines how we collect, process, and safeguard data across our digital touchpoints and client engagements.

2. Information We Collect

We strictly collect information necessary to deliver enterprise services, including:

  • Direct Inquiries: Contact information (name, business email, organization, phone number) provided via our consultation forms.
  • Enterprise Data: System parameters, API configurations, and architectural schemas shared under mutual non-disclosure agreements.
  • Technical Metadata: Anonymized diagnostic logs, browser metadata, and essential session telemetry to ensure platform uptime and security.

3. Use of Data

Data entrusted to AALVINAI Technologies is used exclusively to design, engineer, deploy, and maintain custom solutions. We never sell, rent, monetize, or broker personal or corporate information to third parties.

4. Security & Cryptographic Standards

We implement defense-in-depth engineering standards including AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, strict role-based access control (RBAC), and automated vulnerability auditing across all deployment pipelines.

5. Client Rights & Compliance

In compliance with global data protection principles and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, you maintain full rights to review, rectify, or request the deletion of your personal records at any time by contacting our security team.

For privacy inquiries or compliance auditing, reach out directly to our engineering privacy office at hello@aalvinai.in.
